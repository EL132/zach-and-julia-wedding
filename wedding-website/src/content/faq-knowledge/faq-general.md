# FAQ

FAQ 
Can we bring a plus-one? 
Guest details will be provided with the invitations, which you can expect this winter. 
What is the venue?  
The venue is Maryhill Estate in Glumslöv, Skåne County, Sweden. 
Do you have a registry?  
We are so grateful to all guests for the investment required to attend an international wedding. Please note that your presence is a priceless gift to us. In the event that you would like to provide a gift, we will be setting up registries in the near term. 
How long should we plan to travel? 
We will be hosting events beginning early evening August 14 - early afternoon August 16. We will be in Stockholm the week before the wedding as well (likely beginning August 10th). We will be sharing more details on the weekend and week of events as we continue to plan. 
Where else should we go if we want to make our trip longer? 
We recommend Copenhagen, Norway, and Stockholm, though flights within European countries are often affordable and easy - so the possibilities are endless once you are here! 
What’s the dress code? 
The wedding will be black tie; further details will be provided following the invitations. 
What’s the weather like? 
August in Skåne is lovely, expect temperatures of 20–25°C (68–77°F) with long, light-filled days. Sunset won't come until around 9:00–9:30 PM, giving evenings a magical golden quality. Pack a light layer for cooler nights.
What other clothes should we bring? 
We recommend packing some light jackets / shawls, as well as attire for the various activities available at Maryhill (pool / swimming in the sea, sauna, tennis / padel, hiking). 
When should we RSVP? 
There’s no need to RSVP yet; that functionality will be live after the invitations are sent out. 
When should we book hotel rooms? 
Glumslöv is a small village, so we recommend staying at the venue. We will pre-book all the rooms needed for the two nights of August 14 and 15 and will communicate booking instructions as it gets closer; there is no need to book your room at this time. Please don’t book directly through Maryhill, as we have negotiated discounted rates for our guests. For those who would like to arrive at Maryhill early or stay later, we are also currently coordinating with Maryhill to block the surrounding Friday and Monday nights and will communicate booking instructions once finalized.
What’s up with the fish smoking the pipe? 
Julia’s PopPop, Joe Braig, signed every card with a fish (an avid fisherman) smoking a pipe (a little indulgence here and there). We are honoring Joe’s memory throughout the wedding by making this our de facto logo. Bonus points if on wedding weekend you can spot the many tattoos of the fish smoking the pipe on the siblings and cousins on Julia’s side! 
Can children attend? 
Since the wedding will take place internationally for many guests, we will be hosting an adults-only event aside from close relatives. We appreciate your understanding and cannot wait to celebrate with you!
