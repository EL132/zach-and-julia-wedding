Given recent legislation, you will need to obtain a ETIAS (European Travel Information and Authorization System) waiver for travel to many European countries (including Sweden) beginning in 2027. It is easy to apply and should be valid for three years or until your passport expires. While it is anticipated to process within minutes, we strongly urge guests to apply once it is available (expected to be the last quarter of 2026). Learn more at the official ETIAS site: https://travel-europe.europa.eu/etias

Your Best Bet: Fly into Copenhagen (CPH)

Despite being in Sweden, the easiest and most well-connected gateway to the venue is Copenhagen Airport (CPH) in Denmark. It's the largest hub in Scandinavia and sits just 50 km (about 45–55 minutes by car) from Glumslöv via the famous Øresund Bridge.

Direct flights from the East Coast to Copenhagen (CPH):

New York (JFK/EWR): SAS and Delta operate direct routes, with flight times around 8 hours. This is your easiest connection.
Boston (BOS): Seasonal direct service via SAS; otherwise a single connection through London Heathrow (LHR) or Amsterdam (AMS) gets you there in 10–12 hours total.
Philadelphia (PHL): Typically one connection through a European hub (London, Amsterdam, Frankfurt, or Oslo). Total travel time is usually 11–13 hours.
Atlanta (ATL): One connection through a European hub. Delta connects well through Amsterdam (AMS). Total travel time is typically 12–14 hours.

Step 2: Getting from Copenhagen Airport to Glumslöv

Once you land at CPH, you have three good options for completing the journey.

Option A: Taxi or Rideshare Directly from CPH

~50 minutes | Most convenient

A taxi taken directly from Copenhagen Airport to Glumslöv is straightforward and door-to-door. As with any taxi / rideshare service, price will fluctuate based on season and demand. This is a great option if you're arriving late, have a lot of luggage, or simply prefer not to deal with connections.

Book through:
- Bolt, Lyft or Uber (both operate in the Copenhagen/Malmö area)
- Official Danish taxis at the airport (Dantaxi, 4x35) 

Option B: Rent a Car 

~50 minutes | Most flexible option

All major car rental agencies (Hertz, Avis, Enterprise, Sixt, Europcar) operate directly from the CPH arrivals terminal. Picking up a rental car here gives you total freedom to explore the area during the wedding weekend.

Important notes for driving in Sweden:
- Drive on the right side of the road (same as US). .
- Speed limits are posted in km/h (typically 110 km/h on motorways, 70 km/h on rural roads, 50 km/h in towns).
- Four way intersections operate with the right of way rule, defer to cars approaching to your right. 
- Swedish law requires headlights on at all times, day or night.
- Gas stations are plentiful along the motorway. Most accept US credit cards (although AMEX is often not accepted)

Option C: Train + Taxi

~1 hour 10 minutes total | Comfortable and affordable

Sweden and Denmark have excellent rail connections, making train travel a stress-free alternative.

1. At CPH Airport: Take the Øresund regional train from the terminal's train station (directly beneath the airport — follow signs for "Trains to Sweden"). Trains run approximately every 20 minutes.
2. To Malmö Central Station: ~18 minutes. Tickets can be purchased at the station or via the DSB/Skånetrafiken app.
3. To Landskrona Station: From Malmö Central, take a regional Pågatåget train north and exit at Landskrona (~25–30 minutes). Trains run frequently throughout the day. Occasionally, you can find a train that will take you directly to Landskrona and avoid the stop in Malmö
4. Taxi to the venue which is approximately 3 km from Landskrona station. A short taxi ride (around 10 minutes) will bring you directly to the venue or your accommodation. Taxis are available outside the station or via apps such as Cabonline, Uber, Lyft, or Bolt. As with any taxi / rideshare service, price will fluctuate based on season and demand. 

Booking train tickets: You can book in advance at (https://www.sj.se) or (https://www.skanetrafiken.se). The Øresund crossing portion is operated by DSB/SJ and is very affordable (typically 80–120 SEK / ~$9–13 USD for the full Malmö–CPH leg).

Option D: Bus + Taxi

~ 2 hour 15 minutes total | Comfortable and affordable

Flixbus operates out of Copenhagen airport and allows for simple travel into Sweden while only costing around $20 per ticket. 
Take the FlixBus with a ticket to Lund (~1.5hr bus ride, https://www.flixbus.com/). 
After arriving at Lund, call an Uber/Lyft directly to the venue. It is a 40 minute drive. As with any taxi / rideshare service, price will fluctuate based on season and demand.

A Few Things to Know About Sweden in August

Weather: August in Skåne is lovely, expect temperatures of 20–25°C (68–77°F) with long, light-filled days. Sunset won't come until around 9:00–9:30 PM, giving evenings a magical golden quality. Pack a light layer for cooler nights.

Currency: Sweden uses the Swedish Krona (SEK). Swedish law requires that all places including taxis, shops, and restaurants accept credit and debit card. You do not need to bring cash.

Language: English is spoken very widely throughout Sweden, especially in the Skåne region. You will have no trouble communicating.

Electricity: Sweden uses Type F plugs (the two-round-pin European standard) and 230V/50Hz current. US travelers will need a plug adapter and potentially a voltage converter for older devices.

Phone service: Most major US carriers offer international plans that include Sweden. Check with your provider before you travel. Swedish SIM cards are also affordable and easy to pick up at the airport if you prefer.
