Our Story 

We will be getting married on almost exactly our 6-year anniversary of being together! As many guests know, we met on Tinder (still unclear why Tinder hasn’t sponsored us yet) and both have pretty funny accounts of our first date debrief. After our first date ended at Julia’s college apartment (shoutout 3934), Miley Cyrus was blasting, Liv heard the news, and the conclusion was clear: we had met our match. On the drive back to Malvern, Ainsley got the call: we had found ‘a genuine connection’ after Too Hot to Handle had been in heavy rotation that summer. And that was the beginning of our forever – laughing together across Atlanta, Philadelphia, Manhattan, Stockholm, and Boston. We love and can’t wait to celebrate with you all! 

What’s up with the fish smoking the pipe? 
Julia’s PopPop, Joe Braig, signed every card with a fish (an avid fisherman) smoking a pipe (a little indulgence here and there). We are honoring Joe’s memory throughout the wedding by making this our de facto logo. Bonus points if on wedding weekend you can spot the many tattoos of the fish smoking the pipe on the siblings and cousins on Julia’s side!
