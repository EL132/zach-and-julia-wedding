Our Story 

We will be getting married on almost exactly our 6-year anniversary of being together! As many guests know, we met on Tinder (still unclear why Tinder hasn’t sponsored us yet) and both have pretty funny accounts of our first date debrief. Julia’s version: “After our first date, I burst into Liv’s room at my old college apartment (shoutout 3934), danced around blasting ‘See You Again’ by Miley Cyrus, and declared that I had met the love of my life.” Liv promptly told her to go to sleep :) Zach’s version: “I had been staying with my cousin Ainsley the summer Julia and I met. We spent more time than I want to admit watching Too Hot To Handle together on Netflix. Driving home from my first date with Julia in Ainsley’s car, I remember calling her and saying in a horrible Essex accent that I had ‘found a genuine connection.’” And that was the beginning of our forever – laughing together across Atlanta, Philadelphia, Manhattan, Stockholm, and Boston. We love and can’t wait to celebrate with you all!

What’s up with the fish smoking the pipe? 
Julia’s PopPop, Joe Braig, signed every card with a fish (an avid fisherman) smoking a pipe (a little indulgence here and there). We are honoring Joe’s memory throughout the wedding by making this our de facto logo. Bonus points if on wedding weekend you can spot the many tattoos of the fish smoking the pipe on the siblings and cousins on Julia’s side!
