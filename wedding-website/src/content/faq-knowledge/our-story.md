Our Story 

Zach and I will be getting married on almost exactly our 6-year anniversary of being together! As many of you know, we met on Tinder (not sure why they haven’t sponsored us yet) and both have pretty funny accounts of our first date debrief. After Zach dropped me off at my college apartment (shoutout 3934), I blasted Miley Cyrus, ran into Liv’s room, and declared that I had met the love of my life. Liv promptly told me to go bed. At the same time, as he was driving back to Malvern, Zach called his cousin, Ainsley, and declared he had found ‘a genuine connection’ (they had been watching a lot of Love Island that summer). And that was the beginning of our forever – laughing together across Atlanta, Philly, Manhattan, Stockholm, and Boston. We love and can’t wait to celebrate with you all!
