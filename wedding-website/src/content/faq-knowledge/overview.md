The Venue

Maryhill Estate
Glumslöv, Skåne County, Sweden

Glumslöv is a smaller village on the coast of Skåne — Sweden's southernmost region, known for its rolling countryside, medieval castles, and long summer evenings. The estate sits by the sea and is surrounded by the gentle landscape that makes this part of Sweden so special.
