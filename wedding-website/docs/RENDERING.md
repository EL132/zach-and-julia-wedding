# Maryhill Estate experience

Open the site's navigation, choose **Rendering**, then click **explore**. The entrance contains only that word. After the model loads, a gentle camera move reveals the estate and settles into an overview. The experience fills the viewport; drag to orbit, wheel or pinch to zoom, or use the visible zoom and reset controls. Escape or back returns to the entrance. Arrow keys orbit, +/− zoom and R resets when the scene has focus.

Choose a discovery dot or an entry in **discover** to move closer to the terrace, furnished glasshouse, tennis court, **Julia & Zach** in the cupola, or the sauna. **View estate** returns to the overview. Dragging or zooming interrupts an active camera move.

## Run locally

The site owner starts the server. From `wedding-website`:

```sh
npm install
npm run build
npm run start
```

Visit http://localhost:3000/rendering. Rebuild after changing the page or model before starting the production server again. No Blender installation is needed to run the website. Its existing environment configuration continues to apply to other routes.

## Scene

The revised miniature includes:

- More lawn and mature trees between Hill House and Sugar Club, a curved pool-side accommodation wing, and tennis courts behind that wing, following the supplied aerial reference.
- A layered Hill House terrace with central steps, planted islands, shrubs, flowers, café tables, chairs, scalloped umbrellas, loungers, and the long front pool.
- A furnished pool glasshouse with floral cream sofas, woven loungers, moss and terracotta chairs, brass tables, ceramics, palms, and flowering pots around the existing copper fireplace. Furniture stays outside the indoor pool and hearth footprints.
- Two miniature tennis players with moving legs and racket arms, exchanging a bouncing ball.
- An ivory-dressed Julia with long dark hair and a dark-suited Zach with short dark hair inside the rooftop cupola. They have restrained movement, and Julia's veil moves gently.
- Exactly one physical **J&Z** inscription on the terrace welcome easel, with Julia first.
- Deeper grass and foliage colors, gentle tree-canopy movement, animated pool-surface reflections, and the existing soft landscape edge fade.
- One small gull follows randomized curved paths above the roofs, banks into turns, and alternates flapping with gliding. It leaves the current camera view entirely before entering from the opposite side.
- A hollow cedar sauna has a clear glass door, honey-colored timber boards, tiered benches, a guarded stone heater, a bucket and ladle, linen, and soft amber lighting. Its discovery view looks through the front glazing.
- The glasshouse's copper hearth contains charred logs, glowing embers, and five small flame forms with restrained changes in height and brightness.

The rest of the estate retains its pools, sauna, covered connector, garden pavilion, paths, lamps, and landscaped grounds. Proportions are deliberately adapted to an attractive, readable miniature.

## Assets and implementation

The final GLB contains **12,723,056 bytes (12.13 MiB), 435,184 triangles, 139 meshes, 166 mesh primitives, 97 materials, 182 nodes, and 12 animation clips**. It is self-contained, with no image textures or external decoder dependency. Its animation clips form approximately eight-second loops.

- `public/rendering/maryhill-estate.glb`: browser model, including people and their animation clips.
- `public/rendering/maryhill-estate-poster.webp`: transparent Blender still used when interactive rendering fails.
- `app/rendering/RenderingEntrance.tsx`: one-word entrance and lazy loading.
- `app/rendering/EstateExperience.tsx`: model loading and disposal, canvas, fallback, discovery menu, keyboard and visible controls, motion preference.
- `app/rendering/EstateScene.tsx`: lighting, overview framing, cinematic entrance, discovery camera positions, animation mixer, pool shader movement, canopy movement, and orbit controls.
- `app/rendering/EstateBird.tsx` and `estateBirdFlight.ts`: lightweight procedural 3D gull, curved flight, banking, wing movement, and camera-aware offscreen re-entry. The gull is generated by the browser and is not part of the Blender still or GLB.
- `app/rendering/EstateFire.tsx`: subtle flame scaling, ember-colored local light, and emissive flicker. Flame pivots remain separate in the model.
- `app/rendering/rendering.module.css`: ivory presentation, loading treatment, discovery markers, and responsive controls.

Blender's Z-up coordinates become glTF Y-up coordinates: `[x, y, z]` becomes `[x, z, -y]`. Discovery targets in `EstateScene.tsx` use the latter convention. Update them if a landmark moves.

`Ground_Fade` carries RGBA vertex colors with a gradual alpha perimeter. Preserve its vertex colors and transparent material settings when replacing the model. The viewer disables depth writing for this material and the translucent glass roof. The 32 `Canopy_` parent nodes hold foliage in local coordinates so the trees sway around their own trunks. Preserve those parent/child transforms. Preserve the `Animated_` nodes and exported clips when optimizing the model; the viewer plays all clips together with one animation mixer.

### Motion and resource use

The model and 3D bundle load only after **explore**. The canvas uses demand rendering, but while environmental motion is enabled it explicitly requests each next frame, so animation runs continuously. An active camera journey also requests frames. **Pause motion** stops the environmental animation and interrupts any current camera journey; direct interaction still renders, and a newly selected discovery can still move the camera.

The system's reduced-motion preference disables the cinematic transition and environmental movement by default. Discovery and overview changes become immediate. The visitor can explicitly choose **play motion** for the environment. Hidden tabs stop rendering; returning resumes without advancing animations through the hidden period. Device pixel ratio is capped, the overview adapts to portrait screens, and geometry, material, texture, and environment resources are released when closing the experience.

The gull and fireplace follow the same motion preference. Pausing holds their current poses; with reduced motion enabled from the beginning, the gull remains offscreen. The sauna's warm interior light is steady.

## References and accuracy

This is an original, interpretive miniature based on the 108 photo entries in the [official gallery](https://maryhillestate.com/en/gallery/), the illustrated estate map already in `public/home-page/mary-hill-estate-drawing.png`, and the newer aerial and terrace close-up supplied by the user for V2. The newer aerial guides the relative building spacing, broad intervening lawns, curved accommodation wing, and courts. The terrace close-up guides its richer planting and furniture arrangement.

Strong exterior gallery references include entries 14, 15, 80 and 108; entries 12, 33, 64 and 87 guide the glasshouse and copper hearth; entries 25–27 the sauna; entries 45 and 100 the pool furniture. The gallery index and editable Blender file were delivered separately.

Building proportions and landscape placement remain approximate; this is not a surveyed architectural model. The original gallery photos are reference material and are not embedded or redistributed with the website asset.

## Rebuild the model

The generator and helpers are in `scripts/maryhill`, tested with Blender 5.2.1 LTS:

- `build_estate.py`: site layout, architecture, terrain, landscaping, lighting, animation integration, and GLB export.
- `garden_assets.py`: trees, shrubs, flowers, parasols, loungers, lamps, and garden pavilion.
- `interior_assets.py`: glasshouse furniture, café settings, planters, and the single J&Z sign factory.
- `people_assets.py`: Julia, Zach, tennis players, rackets, ball, veil, and animation loops.
- `sauna_assets.py`: hollow cedar shell, single-plane glass door and window, slatted interior, benches, heater, and small accessories.
- `fire_assets.py`: hearth logs, embers, and separate `HearthFlameRoot_` pivots for the viewer to animate.

From the website root:

```sh
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender --background --factory-startup --python scripts/maryhill/build_estate.py
```

The generator writes the GLB into `public/rendering` and an editable scene plus transparent PNG preview under `.generated/maryhill` (ignored by Git). Use `-- --skip-render` to rebuild only the model and Blender scene, or `-- --output-dir /your/output/folder` to choose an output folder. `--assets-dir` changes the GLB destination. The default script prefers Metal when available; Blender must have a usable rendering device for the preview.

After changing the scene, regenerate the fallback WebP from the rendered PNG so the fallback matches. Keep transparency. Run the website production build after updating the assets. The website itself does not run Blender.
